Answer 1: When the y-axis points down, increasing θ rotates clockwise. When the y-axis points up, increasing θ rotates counterclockwise. This is because a positive 90° rotation maps (1, 0) to (0, 1), which points down or up depending on the coordinate system.

Answer 2: It's better to apply scaling before translation because translating first would also scale the translation offset, causing the object to move unexpectedly when resized.
